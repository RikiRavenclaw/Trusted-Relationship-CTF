Transportation to Hogwarts

Hogwarts coordinates student travel at the beginning and end of each school term. Students should review the travel information and follow the instructions provided by the school.

The Hogwarts Express

The Hogwarts Express is the traditional train service used by students traveling to Hogwarts. The train departs from Platform 9¾ at King's Cross Station in London.

Student travel arrangements

  • Arrive at King's Cross Station with enough time to board.
  • Keep your school identification and travel information available.
  • Follow the instructions of railway staff and Hogwarts prefects.
  • Contact the school if you need assistance with your travel arrangements.
Transportation partner: Hogwarts works with the Hogwarts Express Railway to coordinate student journeys and boarding information.

Railway information

For train schedules, boarding information, and railway services, visit the Hogwarts Express Railway portal.
