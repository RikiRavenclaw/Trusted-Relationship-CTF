Staff Directory

Meet the faculty and staff responsible for teaching, guiding, and supporting Hogwarts students.

Albus Dumbledore

Position: Headmaster

Oversees Hogwarts and guides the school's students and faculty.

Minerva McGonagall

Position: Deputy Headmistress and Professor of Transfiguration

Teaches Transfiguration and assists with school administration.

Severus Snape

Position: Professor of Potions

Teaches students the art of brewing magical potions.

Aurora Sinistra

Position: Professor of Astronomy

Teaches students about stars, planets, and celestial movements.

Filius Flitwick

Position: Professor of Charms

Teaches spells that give objects and people magical properties.

Pomona Sprout

Position: Professor of Herbology

Teaches students about magical plants and their properties.

Cuthbert Binns

Position: Professor of History of Magic

Teaches the history of the wizarding world.

Remus Lupin

Position: Professor of Defence Against the Dark Arts

Teaches students how to defend themselves against dark magic.

Rolanda Hooch

Position: Flying Instructor

Teaches students the fundamentals of flying on broomsticks.

Septima Vector

Position: Professor of Arithmancy

Teaches the magical properties and significance of numbers.

Rubeus Hagrid

Position: Professor of Care of Magical Creatures

Teaches students about magical creatures and how to care for them.

Sybill Trelawney

Position: Professor of Divination

Teaches students various methods of predicting the future.

Bathsheda Babbling

Position: Professor of Ancient Runes

Teaches students to read and interpret ancient magical symbols.
