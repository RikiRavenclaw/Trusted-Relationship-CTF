Staff Directory

Meet some of the faculty and staff responsible for teaching, guiding, and supporting Hogwarts students.

Headmistress / Headmaster

Office: Head's Office, Main Tower

Oversees school operations, student welfare, and academic matters.

Professor of Transfiguration

Office: Transfiguration Wing

Teaches students the principles and practice of transfiguration.

Professor of Potions

Office: Dungeon Classrooms

Oversees potion instruction, ingredients, and laboratory safety.

Madam Pince

Office: Library

Maintains the library collection and assists students with research.

Groundskeeper

Office: Groundskeeper's Hut

Maintains the school grounds and helps care for the surrounding grounds.

Deputy Head

Office: Staff Offices

Supports school administration and coordinates day-to-day student matters.
