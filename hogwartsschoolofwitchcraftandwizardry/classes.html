Classes and Courses

Hogwarts students follow a varied curriculum designed to develop practical magical skills and a strong understanding of the wizarding world.

Charms

Learn spells that add properties or abilities to people and objects.

Transfiguration

Study the complex art of changing the form or appearance of objects.

Potions

Learn how ingredients, precision, and technique combine to produce magical mixtures.

Herbology

Study magical plants, their properties, and their uses.

Defense Against the Dark Arts

Develop knowledge of defensive magic and how to recognize dangerous enchantments.

Astronomy

Observe the night sky and study the stars, planets, and their magical significance.

History of Magic

Explore important events, figures, and developments in magical history.

Flying

Learn the fundamentals of broomstick control, balance, and safe flight.
