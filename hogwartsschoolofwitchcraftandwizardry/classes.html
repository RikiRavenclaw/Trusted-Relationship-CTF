Classes and Courses

Hogwarts students follow a varied curriculum designed to develop practical magical skills and a strong understanding of the wizarding world.

Astronomy

Stars, planets, moons, and celestial movements.

Charms

Spells that give objects or people new properties.

Defence Against the Dark Arts

Protection against dark magic, dark creatures, and curses.

Herbology

Magical plants, fungi, and their properties.

History of Magic

Wizarding history and important historical events.

Potions

Brewing magical potions and understanding ingredients.

Transfiguration

Transforming objects and living things into other forms.

Arithmancy

Magical properties and significance of numbers.

Care of Magical Creatures

Magical creatures and how to care for them.

Divination

Predicting the future through various magical practices.

Muggle Studies

Muggle society, technology, and everyday life.

Study of Ancient Runes

Reading and interpreting ancient magical symbols.
