The Four Houses

Each Hogwarts house values different qualities. Together, they form the spirit and traditions of the school.

🦁

Gryffindor

Values: Courage, determination, and bravery.

Gryffindors are known for standing up for what they believe is right, even when doing so is difficult.

🦡

Hufflepuff

Values: Patience, loyalty, and hard work.

Hufflepuffs value fairness, dedication, and looking out for their fellow students.

🦅

Ravenclaw

Values: Curiosity, wisdom, and creativity.

Ravenclaws enjoy learning, asking questions, and approaching problems in original ways.

🐍

Slytherin

Values: Ambition, resourcefulness, and leadership.

Slytherins are often determined to reach their goals and make the most of their abilities.
