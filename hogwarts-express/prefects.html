Prefect Responsibilities

Authorized Hogwarts student leadership

  • Assist first-year students with boarding and luggage.
  • Help students locate available compartments.
  • Conduct periodic checks of train corridors.
  • Report unusual activity to the train conductor.
  • Assist railway staff with student transportation concerns.
  • Ensure students follow Hogwarts travel policies.
Important: Prefects should report unexpected changes to passenger arrangements to the designated railway employee.
ON THE DAY OF DEPARTURE

Prefect Checklist

Complete these duties before the train leaves King's Cross.

Before Boarding

Arrive early, check in with the conductor, and be available to help younger students.

After Departure

Walk through the carriages, answer student questions, and pass any concerns to railway staff.
