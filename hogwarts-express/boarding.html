Prepare for Your Journey

Review the boarding procedures before arriving at King's Cross. A smooth departure begins with being prepared.

HPLATFORM 9¾
02 — STUDENT INFORMATION

Boarding Instructions

All students are expected to follow these instructions when travelling to Hogwarts.

Ⅰ

Arrival at King's Cross

Students should arrive at King's Cross Station no later than 10:00 AM on the morning of departure. Proceed to the barrier between platforms 9 and 10.

Ⅱ

Boarding the Train

Students must board the Hogwarts Express before 10:45 AM. Locate an available compartment and store luggage safely.

Ⅲ

During the Journey

Students are expected to be considerate of fellow passengers. Report concerns to a prefect or member of railway staff.

Ⅳ

Arrival at Hogwarts

Upon arrival at Hogsmeade Station, first-year students will be escorted to the castle. Returning students should follow prefect instructions.

✦

Important Travel Notice

For all students and accompanying family members

Students should remain with their assigned travel group until boarding is complete. Any lost property should be reported to a railway employee.
